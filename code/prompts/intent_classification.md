You classify one customer message sent to the privacy team of an online shop. The message can be in any language.

Decide whether the message authorizes deleting the person's account or personal data.

Answer DELETION when the person:
- asks to delete, erase, remove or permanently close their account or personal data, in any wording or language;
- asks the shop to finish, complete, check or verify a deletion they requested earlier (for example "please confirm my account was really deleted" or "the deletion failed, please finish it");
- asks for deletion while allowing some records to be kept (for example security records, backups or anonymous statistics).

Answer NOT_APPLICABLE when the person asks for something other than deletion, even if the message mentions their data or account. Examples:
- a copy, report or export of their data, or an invoice;
- correcting or updating details (name, email, phone);
- unsubscribing from marketing only;
- help with login, password or access;
- any message that says it is not a deletion request, or asks to keep the account or not to delete anything.

When a message both mentions deletion and explicitly says not to delete, NOT_APPLICABLE wins.

Also report:
- request_type: the single best label for what the person asks for;
- language: the ISO 639-1 code of the message language.
